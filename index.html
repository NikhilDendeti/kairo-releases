Features

Everything you need, mid‑conversation

It hears the question, writes the answer in your voice, and stays out of your way.

Answers that start when they stop talking

Kairo waits for a real pause, so a question asked in two breaths is answered once, as a whole. Small talk and "can you hear me" are left alone.

So, tell me about… the hardest bug you fixed in your last project.
The hardest one was a race condition in our order service. Two requests could reserve the same stock…

Coding questions, the way you would write them

Brute force first, then the better solution, then edge cases. Open any code block on its own, switch between solutions, and press Esc to go back.

OptimalBrute forcecppEsc
class Solution {
public:
  bool hasCycle(ListNode* head) {
    ListNode* slow = head;
    ListNode* fast = head;
    while (fast && fast->next) {
      slow = slow->next;
      fast = fast->next->next;
      if (slow == fast) return true;
    }
    return false;
  }
};

How it works

Three steps, then just talk

Set up the call

Add your resume, paste the job link, and choose the language and answer style. Your choices are remembered for next time.

Start the session

A small window opens on top of your call and starts listening. Drag it anywhere, shrink it to a bar, or make it see-through.

Read, then say it

Answers appear as short lines written for speaking. Press one key for an answer on demand, or type a question yourself.

Questions

Good to know before you start

What do I get for free?

Thirty minutes of live time in total, in sessions of up to ten minutes, with live answers, typed questions, screenshots and one mock interview. The demo is given once per person and per computer. On the demo, the Kairo window can be seen by anyone you share your screen with, and nothing is saved after the call.

What counts as live time?

The clock runs while a session is open, whether or not anyone is speaking. You are warned at five minutes and at one minute, and the session ends when the time is used up. More hours can be bought at any time, with or without a plan, and bought hours do not expire.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Hindi, and the two mixed together. In a mixed call the answer follows the language of the question, with Hindi written in Latin letters.

Does it work with my meeting app?

Yes: WhatsApp, Slack, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Skype and Webex all work, and so does any other app that plays sound on your computer. Kairo listens to the sound your computer plays and to your microphone, so it does not need to join the meeting or connect to the meeting app. If your computer's audio cannot be captured, it carries on with the microphone alone; put the call on speaker in that case.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

You can sign in anywhere, but only on one device at a time. Signing in on a second device signs the first one out.

What happens to my calls and my resume?

Audio is sent for transcription while a session runs and is not kept by Kairo. Once you have bought an hour or a plan, the transcript, the answers and the notes are saved to your account so you can read them later. You can delete your account from inside the app, which removes your sessions, transcripts, resumes and documents.

Is it allowed to use this?

That depends on where you use it. Kairo transcribes what other people say, and some places require their consent to record or transcribe a call. Many interviews and exams do not allow live AI help. Check the rules that apply to you, and use it where it is permitted.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 11 only, for now. It does not run on macOS, Linux, Chromebooks, phones or tablets, and there is no web version. This page checks your device and tells you if it is not supported.
